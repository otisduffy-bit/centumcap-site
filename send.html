How do I add or change a file?
  1. Upload the file to your Centum folder in OneDrive.
  2. Click the three dots next to it, then Share, then change the setting to Anyone with the link can view, and click Copy link.
  3. In GitHub, open library.js in the centumcap-site repository and click the pencil.
  4. Type the file's name between the quotes after title, a short description after note, and paste the link after url.
  5. Click Commit changes. The page updates in about a minute.
