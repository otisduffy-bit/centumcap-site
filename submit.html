What We Place

If your deal checks these boxes, we want to see it.

Deal types

  • Assignments of purchase contracts
  • Off-market sales direct from owners
  • LP equity, with a preferred equity option
  • Note sales, performing and non-performing
  • REO, lender-direct

Asset types

  • Industrial and flex
  • Medical, dental, veterinary, and medispa real estate
  • Multifamily
  • Retail and single-tenant net lease
  • Office, hospitality, land, and self-storage

Structures

  • Sale-leasebacks
  • Seller financing and earn-outs
  • 1031 exchange buyers and sellers
  • Debt and equity placed together

What stays private

  • Your contract price and minimum acceptable price
  • Your name, until the buyer is registered
  • The address, until the buyer signs the NCNDA

How we are paid

  • The seller pays, only when the deal closes
  • Fees are agreed in writing before we post
  • Buyers owe us nothing

Brokers

  • Brokers are welcome to submit for their clients
  • Fee participation by written agreement, with a valid license
  • Tell us if the deal can go direct to buyers or only through brokers
