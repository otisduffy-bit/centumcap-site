The Problem We Solve

Your team should be building relationships, not getting buried in manual work.

Commercial real estate businesses spend enormous amounts of time on prospect research, data collection, outreach, CRM administration, follow-up, underwriting support, and transaction coordination.

These activities are necessary, but they consume time that could be spent sourcing opportunities, developing relationships, and closing business.

Centum helps businesses identify which processes can be automated, deploy AI-powered workflows, and give their existing teams more capacity.

Traditional operating model

  • Manual prospect research.
  • Disconnected databases.
  • Repetitive data entry.
  • Inconsistent follow-up.
  • Fragmented communication.
  • Manual reporting.
  • Limited business development capacity.

AI-enabled operating model

  • Automated prospect research.
  • Enriched and organized contact data.
  • Intelligent workflow automation.
  • Consistent follow-up systems.
  • Centralized relationship management.
  • Automated reporting.
  • Greater capacity for existing teams.

Not every task can or should be automated. The goal is to take repetitive work off your team's plate, with people reviewing and approving what matters.

Centum helps commercial real estate firms increase business development capacity, automate repetitive operations, and execute more efficiently through AI-powered systems and human oversight.

Example Applications

What could your business automate?

Example 1

Brokerage business development

A brokerage wants to increase the number of property owners and investors it reaches. An AI-powered workflow identifies target companies, researches decision-makers, organizes contacts, drafts personalized outreach, and routes responses to the brokerage team for review.

Example 2

Acquisitions

An acquisition firm wants to identify properties that fit a defined investment strategy. AI workflows help research property information, organize potential opportunities, screen them against acquisition criteria, and prepare preliminary summaries.

Example 3

Investment sales and capital markets

A firm wants to improve its CRM and investor relationship management. Automated workflows organize contacts, identify investment criteria, track communications, and flag relevant follow-up opportunities.

Example 4

Operations

A CRE company wants to reduce manual administrative work. Automations help coordinate documents, track milestones, route approvals, update records, and generate reports.
