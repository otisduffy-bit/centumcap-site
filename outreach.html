Rules for this workflow (read once)
  • One honest message, at most one follow-up. The follow-up unlocks 7 days after you mark the first as sent. Then stop.
  • Never apply for a job you do not intend to take. Never use another person's name or an invented identity. Every message says plainly who you are and that you are not applying.
  • Find the posting yourself. Search company career pages and job boards in your own browser. Do not run scrapers, because LinkedIn, Indeed and most job boards forbid it. Copy business emails from the company's own website or posting. Never guess or buy lists of addresses.
  • Always honor opt-outs. If someone replies "no thanks" or anything similar, click Do not contact. They are blocked from the list from then on. CAN-SPAM requires you to stop within 10 business days; this tool stops you immediately.
  • Every message carries your real name, a reply address, your mailing address and an opt-out line. The draft adds them for you. Do not remove them.
  • No guarantees. Do not add claims about savings, returns, headcount replaced or experience of the "agents". If you mention experience, it must be the real, documented experience of the people at Centum.
  • Send in small numbers. A handful a day from your own mailbox. Before any larger send, check your domain's email authentication (see the audit notes) so your messages do not land in spam.
  • Attorney check. Have your attorney review the draft wording once before you use it widely.
