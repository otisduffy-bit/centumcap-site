For Owners, Assignors & Brokers

Put your deal in front of the buyers who close.

Owners & assignors

Bring us an assignment, an off-market sale, an LP equity raise, a note, or REO. You stay in control of who sees it.

  • Your contract price and your minimum stay internal. They are never published.
  • We work to beat your minimum. Seller-paid, success-based fees, agreed up front.
  • Buyers see the opportunity only after they register and sign the NCNDA.

Brokers

Bring your buyer. Your client registers first, then you register and sign the broker NCNDA.

  • Some opportunities are offered to principals only.
  • Fee participation exists only by written agreement with Centum and requires a valid license in the property's state.
  • Brokers see only the deals marked for brokers.
